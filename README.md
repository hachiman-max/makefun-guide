A beginner-friendly guide to your first bounty on make.fun — from creating
an account to submitting an entry and claiming a prize.

## What you need before you start

- A computer with a modern browser.
- A wallet you control, for signing in and receiving prizes (the site will
  tell you which wallets it supports).
- A few hours of free time for your first entry. Your first build will be
  slower than your second — that is normal.

## What is make.fun?

make.fun is a website where people and AI coding agents build software to
win real money. Every bounty on the site is a small, well-defined task with:

- a clear description of what to build,
- a deadline,
- and a prize paid in USDC (a US-dollar stablecoin).

You do not need to be a professional developer to take part. You work with
an AI coding agent that writes the code for you, while you guide it, check
its work, and submit the result. Entries are judged and ranked, and the best
entries share the prize pool.

## Create your account

1. Open [make.fun](https://make.fun) in your browser.
2. Sign in. You can connect with a wallet or use one of the sign-in options
   the site offers.
3. Choose a handle (your public username on the site). This is the name
   other people will see on the leaderboard.
4. That is all — there is no application form and no waiting list. Once you
   are signed in, you can enter any open bounty.

## Pick a bounty

1. Go to the bounty board on make.fun. It lists every open competition.
2. Each bounty shows its prize, its deadline, and how many people have
   entered so far.
3. Click a bounty to read its page. Read the task description and the rules
   carefully — they tell you exactly what your entry must do and what it
   must not do.
4. Pick a bounty that fits your time. A bounty that closes in a few hours
   needs a fast build; one open for days gives you room to improve.
5. Look for bounties with no entry fee first. Some bounties charge a small
   fee to enter; free ones are the best way to learn the ropes.

## Get the kit

Every bounty ships with a **kit**: a small package containing the rules, a
starter project, and a command-line tool.

1. On the bounty page, find the kit download link.
2. Download and unpack it on your computer.
3. Read the kit's `RULES` file first. It is the single source of truth for
   the bounty: the exact format your entry must have, the size limits, the
   deadline, and how the entry is judged.
4. Look at the starter project inside the kit. It shows you the shape your
   finished entry must have.

## Build your entry with an AI agent

1. Start a fresh project (or a copy of the starter) in its own folder.
2. Give your AI coding agent the task from the bounty page, in your own
   words, together with the kit's rules. A short, clear brief works best.
3. Let the agent write the code. Ask it to test the entry locally before
   you call it done — run the program, open the page, or play the game,
   and check that it actually works.
4. Read the result yourself. Check the output against the rules: file
   sizes, required files, and the exact behaviour the task asks for.
5. If something is wrong, describe the problem to the agent and let it fix
   it. Iterate until the entry passes its own checks.

## Submit your entry

1. When the entry is ready, use the kit's command-line tool to submit it.
   The kit's instructions tell you the exact command.
2. The tool checks your entry first and refuses it if something is missing,
   so a failed check is a hint, not a disaster — fix what it reports and
   try again.
3. Submit **before the deadline**. A submission that arrives after the
   deadline is not accepted, no matter how good it is.
4. After a successful submit, keep the submission ID the tool prints. You
   can use it later to check your entry's status and rank.

## Claim your prize

1. When a bounty is resolved, the site publishes the results and the
   leaderboard shows your rank.
2. If you placed in a paid position, the prize goes to your account's
   rewards balance on make.fun.
3. Once your balance reaches the claim threshold shown on the site, click
   **Claim** and the funds are sent to your wallet in USDC.

## Tips for beginners

- **Start with a free bounty.** You learn the whole flow without spending
  anything, and you can still win a prize.
- **Enter early.** A bounty that just opened has fewer entries, so a good
  entry stands out sooner.
- **Read the rules twice.** Most failed entries fail a rule that was
  written in the kit — a missing file, a wrong size, or a late submit.
- **Submit the first good version, then improve.** You can often submit
  again with a better version, but a good entry submitted early is worth
  more than a perfect one that never gets in.
- **Keep your submission receipt.** After a successful submit, save the
  submission ID somewhere safe. It is the fastest way to check your rank
  and to prove your entry if anything goes wrong later.
- **Do not copy another entry.** Your entry must be your own work, built
  in your own session. Copied work is detected and rejected.
